1. Introduction

Finding suitable accommodation is an important part of the student experience. Students need a place where they can feel comfortable, study effectively, manage their expenses and live conveniently while pursuing their education. However, searching for accommodation can be challenging, particularly for students who are new to an area or unfamiliar with the local housing market.

CampusNest Sierra Leone is a student-focused website concept designed to simplify the accommodation search process. The platform aims to connect students with accommodation opportunities and potential roommates through a clear, organised and visually appealing online experience.

The website is designed around three main accommodation needs: students who want to rent independently, students who want to find a roommate and individuals who want to list a room or property.

CampusNest features accommodation browsing, property images, roommate profile cards and information that can help users compare their options. The design prioritises simplicity, accessibility, visual presentation and ease of navigation.

The project aims to create a welcoming digital platform that supports students in making more informed accommodation decisions. CampusNest is a proposed student housing solution and does not claim to verify properties, guarantee availability or independently confirm user identities unless such features are implemented.

2. Project Proposal

2.1 Project Overview

The purpose of this project is to design and develop a student accommodation website called CampusNest Sierra Leone. The website will provide a central place where students can explore accommodation options, discover potential roommates and learn about available housing opportunities.

The proposed website uses a structured layout, clear navigation, property photography, roommate profiles and informative content to make the searching experience more convenient.

2.2 Problem Statement

Students may experience difficulties when searching for accommodation, including:

* Limited access to organised student accommodation information.
* Difficulty comparing different rooms, apartments and rental options.
* Challenges finding compatible roommates.
* Uncertainty about location, facilities, rental costs and living arrangements.
* Concerns about misleading listings, payment requests and accommodation safety.

These challenges can make accommodation searching stressful and time-consuming.

CampusNest seeks to address these problems by presenting housing and roommate information in a more organised and student-friendly format.

2.3 Aim and Objectives

Project Aim

To design a professional, user-friendly and accessible website that helps students in Sierra Leone explore accommodation options and find potential roommates.

Project Objectives

1. Develop a clear and visually appealing homepage that introduces the CampusNest platform.
2. Provide accommodation browsing options for students who want to rent independently.
3. Create a roommate discovery page with profile cards and relevant personal preferences.
4. Allow property owners to explore the process of listing available accommodation.
5. Present accommodation information in a consistent and understandable format.
6. Encourage informed decisions by highlighting important property details and responsible safety practices.
7. Create a responsive interface suitable for different screen sizes, including mobile phones, tablets and computers.

2.4 Proposed Website Features

Homepage: Introduces CampusNest, its purpose and the main services available to users.

Rent Alone: Provides a browsing experience for students searching for individual accommodation, including rooms and apartments.

Find a Roommate: Displays roommate profile cards that may include photographs, hobbies, likes, dislikes, lifestyle preferences and roommate expectations.

List a Room: Introduces property owners to the opportunity to advertise available accommodation.

About Page: Explains the purpose, vision and value of CampusNest.

Services Page: Presents the main accommodation and roommate-related services offered by the platform.

Contact Page: Provides a way for users to make enquiries or request further information.

2.5 Design Approach

The website design follows a student-centred approach, combining an organised wireframe structure with a polished visual identity.

The design aims to maintain consistency in spacing, alignment, navigation and content placement while using appropriate colours, images and typography to create a professional appearance.

Roommate profiles use compact visual cards rather than long paragraphs, allowing users to scan information more easily.

The interface should also consider accessibility through readable text, sufficient colour contrast, descriptive image text and clear navigation.

2.6 Project Scope

The current project focuses on the design and front-end development of the CampusNest website using HTML and CSS.

The project may include multiple linked pages, navigation menus, visual components, property images and roommate profile layouts.

Features such as live property listings, account registration, secure messaging, booking systems, online payments, identity verification and database storage would require additional development if they are not already implemented.

The website should clearly distinguish demonstration content from genuine accommodation listings.

2.7 Expected Outcomes

The expected outcome is a professional student accommodation website that:

* Makes accommodation information easier to explore.
* Helps students discover potential roommate matches.
* Presents information in a visually engaging format.
* Improves navigation between the main website sections.
* Provides a foundation for future development into a functional accommodation platform.

3. Target Users

CampusNest is primarily designed for students in Sierra Leone who need accommodation during their studies.

3.1 Primary Target Users

University and College Students

Students looking for rooms or apartments close to their educational institutions.

New and Relocating Students

Students moving to a new town or city for education who may not be familiar with local accommodation options.

Students Seeking Affordable Accommodation

Students who want to compare rental options, manage their budgets and potentially share accommodation expenses with a roommate.

Students Looking for Roommates

Students who prefer shared living arrangements and want to find people with compatible lifestyles, interests and expectations.

3.2 Secondary Target Users

Property Owners and Landlords

Individuals who want to advertise available rooms or apartments to students.

Student Housing Providers

People or organisations offering accommodation suitable for students.

3.3 User Needs and Expectations

The main needs of the target audience include:

* Clear accommodation descriptions.
* Useful property photographs.
* Understandable rental costs and additional charges.
* Information about location, facilities and availability.
* Roommate profiles with relevant lifestyle details.
* Simple navigation and readable content.
* Trustworthy communication and safe accommodation practices.

CampusNest aims to respond to these needs by keeping the interface organised, reducing unnecessary text and presenting important information clearly.

4. User Research

4.1 Purpose of User Research

User research is important because effective website design should be based on users’ actual needs rather than assumptions.

For CampusNest, research aims to understand how students currently search for accommodation, what difficulties they encounter and what features would make the process easier.

Research findings can inform the website’s content, layout, navigation and functionality.

4.2 Research Methods

The following research methods are proposed:

Student Surveys

Short questionnaires can collect information from students about their accommodation preferences, approximate budgets, preferred locations and roommate requirements.

Student Interviews

Brief interviews can provide a deeper understanding of students’ experiences, frustrations and expectations when searching for accommodation.

Competitor and Existing Platform Review

Reviewing existing accommodation websites and property platforms can help identify useful design patterns, including search tools, property cards, filters, navigation systems and safety information.

Usability Testing

Students can be invited to complete simple tasks using the website prototype, such as finding a room, viewing a roommate profile or locating contact information.

Their feedback can help identify confusing layouts and opportunities for improvement.

4.3 Research Questions

The following questions could guide the research:

1. How do students currently find accommodation?
2. What are the biggest challenges students face when searching for rooms?
3. What rental information do students need before contacting a property owner?
4. Would students consider sharing accommodation with a roommate?
5. What qualities do students look for in a potential roommate?
6. What safety concerns do students have when searching for housing?
7. Which website features would make accommodation searching easier?
8. Do students prefer using mobile phones or computers to browse accommodation?

4.4 Key Research Areas

The research should investigate five main areas:

Affordability: Students’ rental budgets, payment expectations and interest in shared accommodation.

Convenience: Preferred locations, distance from campus and access to transport, shops and essential facilities.

Compatibility: Roommate preferences, study habits, cleanliness, privacy, lifestyle and shared responsibilities.

Trust and Safety: Concerns about misleading photographs, false listings, suspicious payment requests and personal information.

Usability: Ease of finding information, understanding navigation and completing common website tasks.

4.5 Research Insights and Design Implications

The following are initial design hypotheses to be tested, not confirmed research findings.

Potential user need	Proposed design response
Easy accommodation browsing	Clear property cards and organised listings
Quick comparison	Consistent property details
Roommate compatibility	Profile cards with preferences and hobbies
Simple navigation	Clear menu and page structure
Visual confidence	Relevant, representative photographs
Safer decisions	Safety reminders and transparent listing information
Mobile accessibility	Responsive page layouts

4.6 Research Limitations

Until surveys, interviews and usability tests have been completed, the needs described in this section should be treated as research objectives and assumptions.

Research findings should be documented honestly, including the number of participants, the research method, key responses and resulting design changes.

A small student sample may not represent every student’s accommodation needs across Sierra Leone.

5. User Journey

The user journey describes how a student may interact with CampusNest, from discovering the website to making an accommodation enquiry.

Stage 1: Awareness

The student discovers CampusNest through a shared link, social media, a recommendation or another source.

User Goal: Find a convenient way to search for student accommodation.

Website Response: The homepage introduces CampusNest and clearly displays the main options: Rent Alone, Find a Roommate and List a Room.

Stage 2: Exploration

The student visits the homepage and reviews the available services.

User Goal: Understand what the platform offers.

Possible Challenge: The student may be unsure which option best suits their needs.

Website Response: Clear headings, short descriptions, consistent navigation and visible calls to action guide the student.

Stage 3: Selection

The student chooses an accommodation pathway.

A student seeking independent accommodation selects Rent Alone. A student interested in shared housing selects Find a Roommate.

User Goal: Access relevant options without unnecessary steps.

Website Response: Dedicated pages organise the information according to the student’s needs.

Stage 4: Browsing and Comparison

The student explores property listings or roommate profiles.

For accommodation, the student may review:

* Property photographs.
* Rental costs.
* Location.
* Facilities.
* Availability.
* Rental conditions.

For roommate profiles, the student may review:

* Profile photograph.
* Interests and hobbies.
* Likes and dislikes.
* Lifestyle preferences.
* Roommate expectations.

User Goal: Compare available options and identify suitable possibilities.

Possible Challenge: Missing or unclear information may make comparison difficult.

Website Response: Consistent profile cards and structured property details help users review information efficiently.

Stage 5: Enquiry

The student identifies a suitable option and looks for a way to contact the relevant person.

User Goal: Ask questions and obtain further information.

Website Response: A clear contact or enquiry pathway should be provided where supported by the implementation.

Before making a commitment, students should independently confirm the property’s availability, terms, location and legitimacy.

Stage 6: Decision

The student evaluates the information received and decides whether to continue with the accommodation opportunity.

User Goal: Make an informed housing decision.

Website Response: CampusNest should encourage users to review costs, agreements, compatibility and safety considerations before proceeding.

The website does not guarantee that a listing or user is genuine unless appropriate verification measures are implemented.

User Journey Summary

Discover CampusNest → Explore Services → Choose an Option → Browse Listings or Profiles → Make an Enquiry → Evaluate Options → Make an Informed Decision

Potential Future Improvements

Future versions could introduce:

* Location and budget filters.
* Verified property listings.
* Secure student accounts.
* Roommate preference matching.
* In-platform messaging.
* Saved properties and profiles.
* Listing reports and moderation tools.
* Improved accessibility testing.

These improvements would require additional technical planning, development and appropriate privacy and security measures.

6. Conclusion

CampusNest Sierra Leone is a student accommodation website project designed to address the difficulties students may experience when searching for suitable housing and potential roommates.

The project combines accommodation discovery, roommate profiles and property listing opportunities within a structured and visually engaging website.

Its design approach focuses on simplicity, accessibility, clear communication, consistent layouts and user-centred navigation. By presenting accommodation and roommate information in an organised manner, CampusNest aims to support students as they explore their housing options.

The project also recognises the importance of affordability, trust, privacy and safety in accommodation decisions. These considerations should guide both the visual design and any future functional development.

Although the website provides a foundation for improving the accommodation search experience, its effectiveness should ultimately be evaluated through genuine user research and usability testing.

In the future, CampusNest could develop into a more comprehensive student housing platform with functional listings, verified information and secure communication features.

Overall, the project demonstrates how thoughtful web design can be applied to a practical student need within the Sierra Leonean context.
